An overview of selected work across battery materials, electrochemical testing and practical data tools.

## Lower-impact graphite and graphene

My doctoral research examined routes to produce battery-grade graphite and graphene with lower energy use and environmental impact. The work connected processing and materials characterisation with electrochemical performance.

See the review paper, [Rethinking the Roles of Graphite and Graphene in Lithium-Ion Batteries](/publications/).

## Graphite–silicon composite anodes

I investigated graphite–silicon anodes as a way to increase the energy density of lithium-ion batteries, including how processing and composition affect electrochemical behaviour.

## Battery data plotting with Python and R

I’m developing a practical guide to reading, processing and plotting battery cycling data with free tools. [Read the guide](/battery-plotting/).

## Battery materials recycling

I have explored opportunities to recover and reuse materials from end-of-life lithium-ion batteries, with an interest in turning spent graphite into useful feedstock for new applications.
