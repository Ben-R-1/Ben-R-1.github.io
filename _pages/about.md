I’m an Experimental Cell Engineer at Breathe Battery Technologies. I started in August 2026, bringing experience from a PhD in Analytical Sciences at the University of Warwick into my work in industry.

My doctoral research focused on lithium-ion battery anodes, especially graphite and graphite–silicon composites, and on developing more sustainable ways to manufacture and recover battery materials.

My research has brought together materials processing, characterisation and electrochemical testing. I have worked on lower-energy graphite and graphene processing, contributed to battery scale-up projects, and explored how recovered materials can be returned to useful applications.

I continue to build on that research experience in my current role as an Experimental Cell Engineer.

## Research and technical interests

- Lithium-ion battery anode materials
- Graphite and graphite–silicon composites
- Battery materials manufacturing and scale-up
- Electrochemical testing and performance analysis
- Recycling and circular use of battery materials
- Python and R for battery data processing and visualisation

Outside work, I enjoy playing rugby, going to the gym and learning about history.
